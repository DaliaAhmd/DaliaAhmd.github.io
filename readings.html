The Art of Strategy: A Game Theorist's Guide to Success in Business and Life

Book Avinash K. Dixit & Barry J. Nalebuff · W. W. Norton & Company · 2010 Paperback Edition

An introduction to game theory and how strategic thinking shapes everyday decisions and interactions. The book explores how to anticipate other people's actions, understand incentives, make decisions under uncertainty, and think strategically about competition and cooperation. Using examples from business, politics, sports, and everyday life, it shows how game theory can help us better understand the decisions we make and the behaviour of others.
