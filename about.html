About Me

I'm curious about how the world works, why societies change, and what the future might look like. My background is in business and economics, and I've become increasingly interested in the connections between technology, institutions, and people's lives. Lately, I find myself thinking a lot about AI safety and governance: how increasingly capable AI systems might change our world, and how we can make sure they remain safe and beneficial to people. I don't have all the answers, but I enjoy exploring difficult questions, learning from others, and being open to ideas that challenge how I think.

Outside research and technology, I love art and singing, and I want to make room for the things that bring me joy and let me express myself creatively. I created this website as a little corner of the internet where I can share my research, writing, readings, and personal thoughts. Some things here are projects I've worked on; others are ideas I'm still figuring out. I hope this space grows alongside me as I discover new interests, learn from experience, and explore what matters to me. For me, it's not about having everything figured out—it's about staying curious and continuing to learn.
