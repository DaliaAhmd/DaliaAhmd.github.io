About

A little more about my background, interests, and the kinds of questions I’m interested in working on.

Background

My background is in business and economics, with research experience spanning economic history, financial systems, regulation, and data analysis.

During my undergraduate studies, I became interested in understanding how institutions shape economic outcomes. That led me toward research involving historical financial systems, regulatory documents, and large economic datasets.

I have also worked in coordination and communications roles, where I supported students, faculty, events, logistics, and large-scale activities. These experiences made me particularly interested in the operational side of research and organizations.

Current Interests

AI Safety

How increasingly capable AI systems can be developed and deployed safely, including questions around control, evaluations, alignment, and systemic risk.

AI Governance

How institutions, governments, and international actors can coordinate around advanced AI and create effective governance frameworks.

Technology & Policy

The effects of technological change on labor, institutions, state capacity, economic systems, and public policy.

Research & Operations

Helping research teams work effectively through coordination, project management, systems design, and turning complex questions into useful outputs.

What I’m Building Toward

I’m interested in roles that sit between research and operations: understanding complex problems while also helping the people working on them collaborate, organize projects, and produce high-quality work.

Over time, I hope to work at the intersection of advanced AI governance, public policy, and research operations.
