About Me

I'm curious about how the world works, why societies change, and what the future might look like. I enjoy exploring questions without easy answers and connecting ideas from different fields.

My background is in business and economics, which shaped my interest in institutions, financial systems, and how change affects people's lives. I enjoy investigating questions, finding connections, and understanding the bigger picture.

Economics AI Safety AI Governance Research Art & Singing

01 — What I'm Curious About

I'm fascinated by artificial intelligence and what increasingly capable systems might mean for humanity. How can we make AI safe for people rather than a threat?

I'm particularly interested in AI safety, AI governance, and AGI. I want to understand not only what technology can do, but also how we should develop it, govern it, and prepare for the changes it might bring.

I also love exploring political economy, economic history, and human opportunity—especially how technology and institutions shape our lives.

02 — A Little More About Me

Beyond research and technology, I love art and singing. They give me a different way to explore the world and remind me that life is about more than work, productivity, and trying to understand everything.

I'm still learning, discovering new interests, and figuring out where I want to go. I don't need to have everything mapped out. I'd rather stay curious, question my assumptions, and remain open to changing my mind.

03 — Why I Created This Website

I wanted a place to bring together my research, writing, reading, and personal reflections. It's somewhere I can share ideas that interest me and explore questions I'm still trying to answer.

Not everything here represents a final conclusion. Some things are works in progress; others are simply ideas I want to explore. I hope this website grows and changes alongside me.
