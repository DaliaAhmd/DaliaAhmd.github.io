About Me

I'm a curious person who enjoys thinking about how the world works, why things change, and what the future might hold. I love asking questions, exploring new ideas, and sometimes going down a rabbit hole just because something caught my attention.

I studied business and economics, which introduced me to questions about people, society, and the systems we live in. Over time, my interests have grown to include technology, AI, and how all these things shape our future.

Economics AI Safety AI Governance Research Art & Singing

Things I Think About

Lately, I've been thinking a lot about artificial intelligence. What will happen as AI becomes more capable? How might it change our lives? And, most importantly, how can we make sure it is safe and beneficial for people?

These questions have drawn me toward AI safety, AI governance, and AGI. I'm still learning, and I find it exciting that there are so many important questions we haven't answered yet.

I also enjoy exploring economic history, political economy, and the relationship between technology and society. I like seeing how ideas connect and how decisions made today can shape the future.

Beyond Work and Research

There's more to me than research and technology. I love art and singing, and I want to make room for the things that bring me joy, spark my imagination, and let me express myself in different ways.

I'm still figuring things out, discovering new interests, and learning what matters most to me. I don't have everything planned, and I think that's okay. I'd rather stay curious, be open to new experiences, and allow myself to change my mind.

Why I Created This Website

I wanted a little corner of the internet that feels like my own—a place to share my research, writing, reading, and personal thoughts.

Some things I share here are projects I've worked on; others are ideas I'm still exploring. I don't expect to have all the answers. I just want to keep learning, express what I'm thinking about, and look back one day to see how much I've grown.
