About Me

I'm someone who is curious about how the world works, why societies change, and what the future might look like. I enjoy exploring questions that don't always have straightforward answers and connecting ideas from different fields.

I studied business and economics, which shaped my interest in institutions, financial systems, labour markets, and the ways economic and political changes affect people's lives. Through research, I discovered that I enjoy more than working with data: I like investigating questions, finding connections, and trying to understand the bigger picture.

What I'm Curious About

Lately, much of my curiosity has been drawn toward artificial intelligence. I often wonder what increasingly capable AI systems might mean for humanity, how they could change our lives, and how we can make sure they remain safe and beneficial to people.

I'm particularly interested in AI safety, AI governance, and the questions surrounding AGI. I want to understand not only what technology can do, but also how we should develop it, govern it, and prepare for the changes it might bring.

I also remain interested in political economy, economic history, and the relationship between technology, institutions, and human opportunity. I find it fascinating how decisions made today can shape people's lives for decades to come.

A Little More About Me

Beyond research and technology, I love art and singing. I enjoy having interests that let me explore a different side of myself and remind me that there is more to life than work, productivity, and trying to understand everything.

I'm still learning, discovering new interests, and figuring out where I want to go. I don't expect to have everything mapped out. I'd rather stay curious, question my assumptions, and remain open to changing my mind as I learn more.

Why I Created This Website

I wanted a place to bring together my research, writing, reading, and personal reflections. It's somewhere I can document what I'm working on, share ideas that interest me, and explore questions I'm still trying to answer.

Not everything here represents a final conclusion. Some things are works in progress, and others are simply ideas I want to explore further. I hope this website grows and changes alongside me.
